# Kit Contents!

Here are all the parts that come with the kit! It should be enough for any macropad you may want to make.

You're allowed to use other parts, but you'll have to source them yourself on your on money.

Your case must be entirely 3D printed!

_Feel like something should be included? Let me know and it might just make it into the next edition._

![picture of all the kit components](docs/v3/kit-spread.png)

_click on links to check out their datasheets!_

- 1 unsoldered [Seeed XIAO RP2040](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/XIAO-RP2040/)
- 20x through-hole [1N4148 Diodes](https://www.vishay.com/docs/81857/1n4148.pdf)
- 16x [MX-Style switches](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0565/8070/2297/files/SPEC-KS-3Y10B050NW-X1-Yellow_Switch.pdf?v=1675841028)
- 2x EC11 Rotary encoders
- 1x 0.91 inch OLED display (the pin order is GND-VCC-SCL-SDA, MAKE SURE YOUR PCB MATCHES)
- 16x white blank DSA keycaps
- 20x [SK6812 MINI-E LEDs](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/4960/4960_SK6812MINI-E_REV02_EN.pdf)
- 6x M3x16mm screws
- 6x [M3x5mx4mm heatset inserts](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/2255800046543591.html)

---

## Other requirements

In addition to only using the parts above, there's a couple other things you need to be sure of!

Make sure you meet the following:

- Your PCB is smaller or equal to 100mmx100mm
- Your case fits within 200x200x100mm (length / width / height)
- The PCB only uses 2 layers
- Your case only has 3D printed parts, no acrylic or laser cut parts
