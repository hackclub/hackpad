### Will my hackpad come assembled?
Unfortunately not! Not to fear though - assembling a macropad is super easy! Check out the [assembly guide here](/assembly)

### Can I use a microcontroller other than the XIAO RP2040?
Unfortunately not for hackpad! If you want to use a custom microcontroller, you'll have to submit it to one of the many ongoing [hardware programs](https://hardware.hackclub.com)

The XIAO is a great primer and I recommend using it, but it's totally understandable if you want to do something more advanced! We won't stop you :)


### Can I use acrylic/cnc metal/etc for the case/plate?
Currently, only 3D printed cases are supported! Laser cut acrylic may come in the future

### Can I use parts outside of the approved list?
Yes! You are responsible for sourcing it on your own money though. The kit will not be tailored to you
