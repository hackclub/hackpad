# Assembling your macropad!

Here's a short guide on actually assembling your macropad in real life once that's done!

Before anything, congratulate yourself for getting your parts! It's probably surreal to get your hands on these. Good luck with the rest of your build.

### 1. Solder your diodes!

Note the direction of the diodes - they have a little black bar on one side so you can know the polarity! Make sure you don't solder them on backwards

![image](https://cdn.hackclub.com/019ecd27-c342-7cdc-a38c-efdf551b5cbf/paste-1781558395350.png)

Make sure to clip the legs off after - it'll be a lot easier to solder everything else once you do! Wirecutters work best, but scissors work in a pinch.

### 2. Solder your other components!

These are components like rotary encoders, your MCU, RGB LEDs, etc. You'll want to solder them before the switches, since they become really hard to access after.

Constantly check against your CAD to make sure everything lines up, since unsoldering can be pretty difficult!

### 3. Run an electrical test!

You'll want to power on your macropad and test everything works with your firmware! Use a pair of tweezers (or wire, or really anything that conducts) to bridge the two pads of each switch, and make sure it sends a keystroke to your computer!

You'll want to do this _before_ soldering your switches, otherwise it'll be really difficult to make edits after!

### 4. Clip your switches into your plate!

Next, you'll want to clip your switches into your plate! You'll need to do this **before** soldering.

DO NOT FORGET THIS. YOU WILL NOT BE ABLE TO ASSEMBLE EVERYTHING OTHERWISE.

### 5. Assemble your case!

This step will change depending on your design, but it should be relatively simple! Screw everything together

### 6. Submit a demo!

Fill out the [demo form](https://forms.hackclub.com/hackpad-demo) once you're done! It'll get your hackpad on the gallery.
