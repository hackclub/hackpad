# Assembling your macropad!

Here's a short guide on actually assembling your macropad in real life once that's done!

First of all, congratulate yourself for getting your parts! It's probably surreal to get your hands on these.

### 1. Solder your diodes!

Note the direction of the diodes - they have a little black bar on one side so you can know the polarity!
